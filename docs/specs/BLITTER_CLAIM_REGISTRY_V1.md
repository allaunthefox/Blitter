# WebGPU Blitter Claim Registry V1

**Schema:** `mathpunch.blitter-claim-registry.v1`  
**Status:** normative for `BLITTER-*` claims on `feat/webgpu-blitter-occupancy-status`  
**Frozen semantic profile:** `docs/specs/BLITTER_ISA_PROFILE_V1.json` (`BLITTER-ISA-V1`)  
**Authority rule:** this registry narrows prose claims. A benchmark, unit test, successful daemon run, cross-device match, or implementation hash cannot promote a claim by itself. Promotion requires every item in `required_evidence`, its falsifier, and a contract-removal gate.

The status vocabulary is the closed vocabulary in `docs/specs/CLAIM_REGISTRY.md`. These scoped rows are deliberately conservative: evidence added on this branch is recorded as evidence, but a status remains `HYPOTHESIS` or `QUARANTINED` until that evidence is actually executed, hashed, replayed, and admitted by the promotion rule. The frozen ISA profile identifies the intended semantic relation; backend conformance is a separately checked refinement relation and is never inferred from matching hashes alone.

## U192 frozen boundary contract

The authoritative mathematical value is independent of byte encoding. The canonical internal word representation is six unsigned 32-bit limbs:

```text
[w0,w1,w2,w3,w4,w5]   with w0 least significant
value = Σ wi · 2^(32i)
```

The frozen V1 ABI uses one byte `vv l b rrrr`. Every raw byte has a structural decode, matching the ISA discipline, but the V1 transport admits only version `vv=0` and reserved bits `rrrr=0000`. The two order bits therefore admit exactly four canonical profiles:

- `0x00` `le-u32-lsw-first`: `w0..w5`, each limb little-endian;
- `0x10` `be-u32-lsw-first`: `w0..w5`, each limb big-endian;
- `0x20` `le-u32-msw-first`: `w5..w0`, each limb little-endian;
- `0x30` `be-u192-msb-first`: `w5..w0`, each limb big-endian, equivalent to canonical 24-byte big-endian integer form.

There is deliberately **no host-native, auto-detection, or generic mixed mode**. The two mixed cases are accepted only when their exact transform is explicitly tagged. At the ABI boundary, the tag is consumed and the payload is normalized immediately to canonical `[w0..w5]`; downstream arithmetic does not reason about transport endianness. The descriptor must byte-identically re-encode under its admitted tag. Wrong, missing, future-version, reserved-bit, malformed, and noncanonical descriptors fail closed.

The B4 machinery is borrowed as an **exact witness architecture**, not as unrelated braid mathematics. `u192-format-matrix-v1` covers the `2×2` product of limb order × limb-byte order exactly once. A digest-bound verified projection may authorize only the declared representation conversion. It does not prove `U192_ADD_MOD`, `U192_MAX`, merge exactness, or any braid-theoretic statement.

## Frozen-ISA rule

`BLITTER-ISA-V1` is the single semantic source for the scoped blitter relations. WGSL, Rust, C++, Lean, and future substrates are lowerings/projections of that profile. A semantic-profile digest binds which specification was targeted; it is **not** a conformance proof. Conformance requires checked behavior/refinement evidence against the frozen relation, including negative controls and exact source/kernel binding where backend correctness is claimed.

Operational layers remain separate:

- heartbeat is advisory discovery/routing;
- lease/fencing has separate safety/liveness obligations;
- ratchet counts are execution/provenance state;
- environmental jitter, thermal, RAS, and ECC are pre-execution adapter inputs or advisory telemetry under the closed-universe rule, never hidden live arithmetic state;
- image provenance requires image/build evidence, not an ISA hash.

## Promotion rule

No `BLITTER-*` status promotion is permitted unless:

1. all `required_evidence` exists;
2. evidence is executed against the exact current source and frozen-profile hashes;
3. every falsifier/negative control passes;
4. the evidence is independently replayable at the claimed independence tier;
5. a mechanical contract-removal test fails if the evidence artifact is deleted or changed; and
6. the exact scope, trusted base, environment, and untested surfaces remain explicit.

Until then, source/proof/test/profile files may reduce an obligation without changing claim authority.
